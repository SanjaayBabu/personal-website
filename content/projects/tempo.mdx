# The Why

Over the years, I've had a lot of goals — to learn four languages, to learn how to code, to exercise & get in shape, to become an expert coffee brewer, and so on. 

Often, the way these goals die is simply not spending enough time on it. You know you're not serious about & not going to achieve your goal of learning four languages if you spend 0 hours a week in language classes. 

To overcome this & spend enough time on the things I care about, I built an app which encourages proactive, time-based goal setting & time-logging. 

# The What

In its simplest form, Tempo is a proactive time logger. You set goals on how long you want to spend in a certain "zone" or domain each week (on Sunday), and log your time over the week. As you log your time, you can observe the zone bar filling over time.
