## Overview

Founded a project team aimed to mitigate incineration of used plastics in the Singapore Army.

## Partnership with Magorium

Negotiated a partnership with Singaporean green startup Magorium and successfully ran a pilot to collect used plastics in a military camp for upcycling into asphalt.

This is part of a broader effort to reduce waste incineration by 30% by 2030, by redirecting recyclable waste into recycling bins.

## Behavioral Nudges

Used behavior science and "choice architecture" to encourage people to increase recycling bin utilization rates. By redesigning the placement and labeling of bins, we created environments where the sustainable choice became the easy choice.

## What's Next

Collaborating with the SAF Sustainability Office to implement this initiative nationwide across all military camps.
